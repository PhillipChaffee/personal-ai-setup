# Appointments

Running log of medical appointments and what they generate. The weekly
`health-followups` automation reads this whole file, cross-checks it against
the calendar, and maintains the `## Follow-ups` section — keep that heading
exactly as spelled, and keep its items as one checkbox line each. Delete the
rows marked EXAMPLE before your first real run (unticked example items would
count toward the automation's weekly "N items" push).
